Submit a paper resource

Submission is open. Give the house a resource — a paper, a preprint, a page — by its locator: an arXiv, DOI, Hexagon, or viXra identifier, or a plain web address. Every resource carries one address under the house's paper addressing scheme (a profile of phextura Layer 2). The address names the work, wherever it lives; the house verifies the title, assesses the claims, and files its determination under that address.

As you type, the page derives the address your resource will carry. The house's server derives it again on receipt, and the server's derivation is the one recorded.
